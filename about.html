About MajorCarX

Who We Are

MajorCarX is a specialized automotive garage focused on luxury customization, high-performance vehicle tuning, and precision motor care. Founded by passionate auto enthusiasts, our workshop handles everything from everyday high-spec maintenance to full track-ready performance builds.

We pride ourselves on using state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment, proven performance parts, and meticulous craftsmanship to ensure every car leaving our facility meets strict quality and safety standards.

Our Mission

To provide high-performance automotive tuning, custom styling, and reliable maintenance services tailored to the needs of modern car owners and motoring enthusiasts.

Our Vision

To become a leading brand in automotive performance and vehicle styling, recognized for technical expertise, customer trust, and dedication to car culture.

Core Values

  • Quality Craftsmanship: Every modification is tested and executed with precision.
  • Customer Transparency: Honest recommendations, clear pricing, and no unnecessary work.
  • Passion for Performance: Driven by real passion for motorsports and automotive engineering.
