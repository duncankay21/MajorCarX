Who We Are

About MajorCarX

Built by car guys, for car guys. We focus on clean builds, proper tunes, and making cars look and run the way they should.

🏁

Where We Started

MajorCarX started from a simple obsession with cars and custom styling. What began as working on personal projects in a garage turned into a full-time spot for anyone wanting to push their machine past standard factory specs.

⚡

Our Goal

We aren't here to just swap out parts and rush jobs. Our main focus is getting your car dialed in right—whether that's squeezing out better performance, dropping the stance, or sorting out custom aesthetics.

🛠️

Inside the Workshop

Our bays are kitted out with the right diagnostic gear, tuning software, and tools to handle custom exhausts, suspension setups, and engine tuning without cutting corners.

🤝

Built By Car People

We treat every ride that rolls through our doors like it's our own. You get straight talk, honest advice on what works best for your setup, and zero guesswork.

🎯

Our Vision

To keep pushing the local scene forward as a go-to spot for performance tuning and clean builds, known for doing things the right way the first time.

💎

How We Work

  • Clear communication: You'll always know what's happening with your car.
  • Obsessive detail: Because small things make a big difference on a build.
